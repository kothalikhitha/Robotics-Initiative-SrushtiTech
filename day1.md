# Srushti Technologies Robotics Initiative — Day 01

## Topic of the Day (TOD): Fundamentals of Robotics Systems
**Focus:** Robot Components and Degrees of Freedom

### Key Learnings
- **Robot Components:**
  - Manipulator (Arm)
  - Actuators (Motors/Drivers)
  - Sensors (Feedback systems)
  - Controller (Processing unit)
  - End-Effector (Tool/Gripper)

- **Degrees of Freedom (DOF):**
  - Rigid body in 3D space → 6 DOF
    - 3 Translational: X, Y, Z
    - 3 Rotational: Roll, Pitch, Yaw
  - Robots are designed with specific DOF depending on tasks.

### Understanding
- DOF defines how flexible and capable a robot is.
- More DOF increases versatility but also control complexity.
